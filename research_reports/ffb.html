just about milk.

Farm Fresh has moved beyond the early-stage Malaysian dairy story. FY2026 revenue exceeded RM1.1bn and PATAMI reached about RM130m, while the group continues to expand its domestic manufacturing footprint, livestock base and regional operations.

Core view. We remain constructive because FFB combines a recognised consumer brand, vertical integration, manufacturing scale and several identifiable growth levers. The stock is no longer cheap on a simple historical multiple, so the next leg must be earned through visible earnings growth.

What we like

  • Established Malaysian dairy brand with growing distribution.
  • Vertical integration across farms, processing and branded products.
  • Ice cream capacity provides a meaningful new category opportunity.
  • Growing herd and domestic manufacturing capacity support volume expansion.
  • Philippines and Cambodia provide longer-duration regional optionality.

What keeps us cautious

  • Input-cost volatility can pressure gross margins.
  • Large expansion projects create a cash-flow/capex mismatch before returns mature.
  • Regional businesses are still less proven than the Malaysian core.
  • Premium valuation leaves less room for execution mistakes.
  • Growth must translate into ROIC and free cash flow, not just revenue.

A vertically integrated

08 / RISKS & THESIS KILLERS

The biggest risk is not
lack of demand. It is poor capital returns.

Primary risks

  • Input costs: milk powder, packaging, energy and agricultural costs can pressure margins.
  • Capex intensity: new factories, farms and equipment consume cash before earning mature returns.
  • Execution: regional expansion can destroy returns if utilisation remains low.
  • Competition: branded dairy and chilled categories remain competitive.
  • Valuation: premium stocks can derate quickly when growth expectations fall.

Hard thesis killers

1. Revenue growth continues but incremental margins deteriorate materially.
2. New capacity fails to generate acceptable returns on invested capital.
3. Regional expansion requires repeated capital injections without a credible path to profitability.
4. Free cash flow remains structurally weak despite earnings growth.
